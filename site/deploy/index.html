_faq

Questions

Does it copy my plugins?

No. Plugin licenses are tied to each computer, so you reinstall them from the maker. Freighter tells you exactly which ones, and flags tampered copies.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your computers and your own drive.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Freighter is free.
