_freighter: start here
======================

Freighter moves your FL Studio and Ableton studio to a new computer: projects, presets, every sample your
projects use, and plugins that are only files. Nothing you do with it leaves your computer.


1. Unzip it
   Windows: right-click the zip, choose Extract All, then open the new folder.
   Mac:     double-click the zip, then open the "freighter-mac" folder.

2. Open Freighter
   Windows: double-click freighter.exe.
            If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
            (Freighter isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't know it.)
   Mac:     double-click freighter.
            The first time, macOS says it can't open it. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
            scroll down, click Open Anyway, then double-click freighter again.

3. Use it
   A window opens with a numbered menu: type a number and press Enter.
   Type 9 to open Freighter in your web browser instead (a private page on your own computer).
   Keep the window open while you use Freighter. Closing it stops Freighter.


What's in this folder
   freighter        moves your studio (start here)
   manifest         lists the plugins on this computer
   crate            sorts drum kits and sample packs into one library
   WINDOWS-TEST.md  a step-by-step test, for test builds
   CHANGELOG.md     what's new
   LICENSE          free software (GPL-3.0)

Something wrong or confusing?
   Type r in the menu, or open https://github.com/chasubowl-creator/freighter/issues/new/choose
